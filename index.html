Max Lapin

I'm a Member of Technical Staff at Reka AI, where I work on training world models. Most recently I worked on Rho-1, an omni model that understands and generates text, images, and video, reasons over them, and takes actions within a single neural network.

Previously, I was a research scientist at Stability AI working on image and controllable video generation; before that, I spent six years at Amazon working on computer vision for robotics and visual search.

I did my PhD at the Max Planck Institute for Informatics and Saarland University with Bernt Schiele and Matthias Hein.

Research

My research focuses on generative models that understand, generate, and reason with multimodal data, including language, images, video, and 3D. I'm especially interested in where 2D and 3D vision meet: novel view synthesis, camera control, and world models that keep a consistent 3D scene. Representative work is highlighted.

Rho-1
Rho-1: An Omni Model That Understands, Simulates, and Acts
Reka AI team
I worked on pre-training and world-model research.
Research preview, 2026
blog post

Instead of a pipeline of specialist models, one network handles text, images, video, and robot actions as tokens in a single context. It is trained from scratch with next-token prediction and flow matching.

Stable Cinemetrics
Stable Cinemetrics: Structured Taxonomy and Evaluation for Professional Video Generation
Agneet Chatterjee, Rahim Entezari, Maksym Zhuravinskyi, Max Lapin, Reshinth Adithyan, Amit Raj, Chitta Baral, Yezhou Yang, Varun Jampani
NeurIPS, 2025
project page/ arXiv

What would a cinematographer ask of a video model? 76 filmmaking controls, 0.25M expert annotations of 20K videos from 10+ models, and a VLM judge that beats zero-shot baselines.

Animate a portrait from any driving video. Masked facial regions carry the motion, and cross-identity training keeps the driver's appearance from leaking through.

Top-k losses (TPAMI)
Analysis and Optimization of Loss Functions for Multiclass, Top-k, and Multilabel Classification
Max Lapin, Matthias Hein, Bernt Schiele
IEEE TPAMI, 2018
project page/ arXiv/ code/ losses demo/ simplex demo

One framework for multiclass, top-k, and multilabel losses: which are calibrated for top-k error, and how to optimize all of them with SDCA.

Top-k Error (CVPR)
Loss Functions for Top-k Error: Analysis and Insights
Max Lapin, Matthias Hein, Bernt Schiele
CVPR, 2016
project page/ arXiv/ code/ losses demo

If you are graded on top-5 error, train for it. We introduce the smooth top-k SVM and top-k entropy losses, which improve the top-k accuracy they target across 11 datasets.

Top-k SVM
Top-k Multiclass SVM

A multiclass SVM that only asks for the right answer to be among the top k, trained with a fast projection onto the top-k simplex.

SVM+
Learning Using Privileged Information: SVM+ and Weighted SVM
Max Lapin, Matthias Hein, Bernt Schiele
Neural Networks, 2014
project page/ arXiv

Weighted SVM can always replicate an SVM+ solution. SVM+ turns out to be a weighted SVM in disguise, so privileged information is best spent deciding which training examples matter.
