libsdca

Accurate and Fast Classification Losses for SDCA and Neural Networks

Technical report GitHub PyPI crates.io Losses demo Simplex demo Cite

libsdca is an open-source library of classification losses. It provides them as loss layers for neural networks in PyTorch and JAX, and as objectives of a stochastic dual coordinate ascent (SDCA) solver for linear and kernel classifiers on fixed features. The losses are numerically accurate and fast. libsdca is also the reference implementation of the top-k SVM and top-k entropy losses (NeurIPS 2015, CVPR 2016, TPAMI 2018).

The two figures below show the speed of libsdca 0.4 in training on a GPU against the compiled losses of PyTorch and JAX. They are interactive and come from the technical report, which also covers the numerical accuracy, the SDCA solver, and the limitations.

At a glance

  • Losses. Cross-entropy with the options of torch.nn.CrossEntropyLoss, the multiclass hinge, top-k cross-entropy and top-k hinges, multilabel cross-entropy and hinge, knowledge distillation, and proximal losses.
  • Loss layers. Each loss is one fused kernel for CUDA and for the CPU. It reads the logits and writes the loss and its gradient without intermediate arrays. PyTorch and JAX share the same kernels.
  • Solver. The SDCA solver trains linear and kernel models. It reports a fit as solved only when the duality gap confirms the requested tolerance.
  • Interfaces. PyTorch, JAX with the signatures of Optax, NumPy, scikit-learn, Rust, C++11, and C.
  • Formats. Logits in fp16, bf16, fp32, and fp64.
  • Release and license. Version 0.4, November 27, 2026, MIT license.

What the report covers

The technical report gives the definitions, numerical methods, kernel design, benchmarks, and limitations.

  • Numerical accuracy. Standard cross-entropy rounds the gradients of confident predictions to zero. libsdca keeps them correct at 4–8 times larger margins, and the report tests every loss against 100-digit references (Section 2).
  • Runtime and memory. The loss layers are benchmarked on a GPU and on a CPU against eager PyTorch, torch.compile, jax.jit, and Liger-Kernel, and libsdca is faster in most comparisons. The SDCA solver fits multiclass SVMs up to 20 times faster than LIBLINEAR (Section 3).
  • Training. Accurate losses remove the loss spikes of standard cross-entropy at high confidence and the drift of standard distillation, and a multiclass hinge groks modular arithmetic at margins near one (Section 4).
  • Appendices. The derivation of every loss in one form, the numerical problems and their fixes with the known limits, the benchmark protocol and results, and the full training experiments.

Quick start

pip install 'libsdca[sklearn,torch,jax]'    # wheels for Linux x86-64, macOS arm64
cargo add libsdca                           # Rust crate
import torch, libsdca
from libsdca.torch import CrossEntropyLoss, TopKCrossEntropyLoss

criterion = CrossEntropyLoss()              # drop-in for nn.CrossEntropyLoss
loss = criterion(net(x), y)
loss.backward()

top5 = TopKCrossEntropyLoss(k=5)            # or TopKHingeLoss(k=5, gamma=1.0)

# SDCA solver: stops at a relative duality gap of 1e-4
model = libsdca.fit(X, y, libsdca.MulticlassHinge(), tol=1e-4)
